\statement{Early Career Contributions}
My previous research has focused on instrumentation development for ground based optical telescopes, target-of-opportunity searches for electromagnetic counterparts of gravitational wave events, and constraining dark energy using electromagnetic and gravitational wave data.
% . First, I commissioned the focal plane array of the LSST Camera and led testing and integration of multi-object and integral-field spectroscopic instruments, building a foundation in the hardware underlying modern survey astronomy. Second, I led searches for electromagnetic counterparts to gravitational-wave mergers using the Vera C. Rubin Observatory, the Dark Energy Camera, and the Prime Focus Spectrograph. Third, I measured the Hubble constant using the dark siren method, combining gravitational-wave data with the Dark Energy Legacy Volume Explorer galaxy catalog. Together, these contributions trace a path from instrumentation to multi-messenger discovery to cosmological inference.
