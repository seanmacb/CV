My career has spanned different methods, fields, and research disciplines in observational astrophysics, recently converging toward using gravitational waves to constrain the evolution of the universe on the largest scales. Despite a broad portfolio of activities, I have retained a pointed focus on building innovative instrumentation to gain a deeper understanding of the universe, leveraging emerging probes of the universe to better understand the nature of energy and matter on the largest scales, and mentoring the next generation of passionate scientists. 
In this vein, I envision my ideal role as one where I can honor each pillar of my ethos - first, through postdoctoral research and leadership in mid-size and large scientific collaborations, but ultimately as a faculty member at an institution where I can lead a group of eager junior scientists to collaborate with peers on future cosmic experiments.
In this proposal, I will describe how my past research experience has comprehensively prepared me to continue exploring the cosmic frontier as a \Position, and how my future contributions will contribute positively to the research and collegial environment of \Institute.
